A short scenario-based course · about 5 minutes

AI can help with the task. Does it help you learn?

Step into a teacher’s day and make four decisions about a student using generative AI to prepare for a maths test. See the consequences of each choice as the story unfolds.

✦

The situation

It is Monday morning. Jakub, a first-year secondary-school student, has a maths test on linear equations next week. He has been practising with a general AI chatbot and says, “I get the answers right now, so I think I’m ready.” You want to help him use AI without mistaking a polished answer for lasting learning.

Decision 1 of 4 Score: 0
ONE THING TO TAKE WITH YOU

The idea to keep

The useful question is not simply whether AI was used. Ask what the student learned, what they can explain independently, and how the activity helped them get there.
